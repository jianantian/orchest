# Competitor Scan — Workflow Efficiency Tools

Author: Priya S., Product Marketing
Date: 2026-03-22

Quick scan of the three tools prospects mention most often when comparing against
Loom, based on sales call notes and public pages.

## Flowbase

- Positioning: "workflow automation for ops teams"
- Pricing: $28/seat/month, annual only
- Strengths mentioned by prospects: cleaner onboarding wizard, native Slack digest
- Weaknesses mentioned: no offline mode, weaker export tooling

## Tempo

- Positioning: "the calm way to run operations"
- Pricing: **TBD** — Tempo relaunched their pricing page last week and the new tiers
  aren't public yet; sales has asked their rep but hasn't heard back. Don't guess a
  number for this in anything customer-facing.
- Strengths mentioned by prospects: fastest time-to-first-value in demos
- Weaknesses mentioned: limited integrations, small company (renewal risk concern
  came up twice)

## Nudge

- Positioning: "lightweight reminders and checklists"
- Pricing: $12/seat/month, monthly or annual
- Strengths mentioned by prospects: cheap, simple
- Weaknesses mentioned: not really a Loom substitute — more of a to-do list with
  notifications; prospects who mention it are usually not a real competitive threat

## Takeaway

Flowbase is the closest real competitor on features; Tempo is the one to watch given
demo speed, but the pricing gap means we can't finish a head-to-head pricing
comparison until their new tiers are public.
