# Support Ticket Themes — Q3 Rollup

Author: Marcus T., Support Lead
Date: 2026-04-10

## Volume and top themes

327 tickets tagged `loom` in Q3, up 12% quarter over quarter. Top three themes:

1. **Onboarding friction** (41% of tickets) — users get stuck connecting their first
   data source and file a ticket instead of retrying. Several tickets mention giving
   up before finishing setup.
2. **Slow export on large workspaces** (24%) — CSV export times out above ~50k rows.
3. **Confusing permission model** (18%) — admins can't tell who can see what.

## Retention context (from the raw funnel pull, not the finance cut)

Support pulled the **unreconciled** funnel numbers directly from the product
analytics warehouse to cross-check churn complaints against actual retention:
referral-channel 30-day retention in Q3 comes out to **35%**, not the ~42% floating
around in the growth deck. The gap looks like it's the onboarding-drop-off accounts
that finance excludes from their reconciled cut — but that's a guess, nobody on
support has confirmed it with growth analytics yet.

If onboarding friction is really costing 7 points of referral retention, fixing (1)
above is probably worth more than the Q4 renewal push discussed elsewhere.
